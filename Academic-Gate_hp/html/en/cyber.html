Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Hands-on network defense, learned through attack and defense

A series that teaches the attack and defense techniques used in practice, hands-on, with Kali Linux (the attacking side) and Parrot OS (the defending and target side). You will understand how attacks work at the packet level, and work all the way through to designing defenses that protect a small business.

  • Attack — Kali Linux
  • Defense — Parrot OS

How to read this series

Each topic is learned as a pair: “what the attacker does” and “how the defender detects and stops it”. Real traffic is visualized with Wireshark / tshark so that you get a feel for why an attack works and what it looks like. For techniques that could be used directly to break in, such as brute force and exploits, the hands-on work is done from the side of understanding the principle, detecting it in logs and packets, and defending against it (because the defender’s point of view is the most useful one, both in practice and for learning).

Only do the exercises in an environment you control (your own virtual machines or lab). Scanning or breaking into other people’s systems or networks without permission is against the law in most countries (in Japan, for example, the Unauthorized Computer Access Act).
